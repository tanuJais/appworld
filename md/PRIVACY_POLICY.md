# Privacy Policy for Vedic Maths Mastery

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Vedic Maths Mastery is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or network-based tracking.

## Data handling

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Learning progress, learner profiles, and practice history are stored only on the user's device using local storage and a local database. This information is not sent to the developer or to third parties.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all learning data remains on the device, no personal information is transmitted to us.

## Data deletion

Users can delete a learner profile and its progress in the app. Users can also use Reset All Progress in Settings. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data according to the device operating system's behavior.

## Changes to this policy

If the app adds account, analytics, advertising, cloud-sync, or other data-collection features, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
